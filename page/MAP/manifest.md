**What are our policies?**
- Slash tariffs, subsidies, taxes and price controls - we want your money to belong to you, not anyone else.
- Free market - in addition to no tariffs, we promise to allow *total* free trade.
- Abolishment of fishing quotas and fishing subsidies - we believe authoritarian fishing quotas violate your right to your own property
- Absolute free speech - we believe every living being should be entitled to have their opinions, and a right to debate and criticise.
- Legalisation of recreational drug use and gun ownership - the government should not control what you do in your free time. Your freedom should always your freedom, and we will keep it that way.
- Developmentalism - we aim to only focus on developing Arkiden's rich economy, heritage and culture. We will stay as neutral as possible in the case of civil unrest, and non-interventionalist if foreign conflicts become an issue.
