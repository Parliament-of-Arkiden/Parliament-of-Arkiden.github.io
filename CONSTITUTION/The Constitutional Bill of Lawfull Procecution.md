# **Section 1: Citizen's suing**
§1 If a citizen wishes to sue an individual or coroporation their nearest court must accept the case and schedule a hearing in such the citizen's lawyers may present evidence including but not limited to witnesses & media following such an appointed judge may either schedule another hearing, dismiss the case or schedule a trial.

# **Section 2: Appointed Judges**
Appointed judges are members of society who have received their rights arcording to the constitutional bill of rights section 0. They must be approved by the ministers scruity council & the presidents privy council the scrutiny council *must* accept them no matter their current position in the government. Unlike lawyers judges must read the full constitution & all passed laws following such a test will be held by the unappointed council of parliament (7 members)

# **Section 3: Lawyers**
§1 Any member of society may work as a lawyer with no expertise in the field.
§2 If a citizen seeks a lawyer the lawyer *must* state their total wins & losses and how many cases they are currently involved in.
§3 State Attorneys are atternoys who have underwent to same process as appointed judges just that they are atternoys who are verified reassuring clients that the attorney is trustworthy 

# **Section 4: Dismissal of Judges & Attorneys**
§1 Judges may only be dismissed by a conclusive vote with 70% of parliament agreeing to dismiss them (any members of parliament currently involved in a legal process are not eligible for participating in such poll)
§2 Attonerys may only be permanently expelled from serving as an attorney by the minister's privy council with a vote concluding with 60% (2/8) of the council voting to dismiss them any members currently involved in any legal process are not eligible for participating in such poll or even discussion.

# **Section 5: Standard legal procecution**
Unless explicitly requested by the plaintiff they will receive a judge for their trial & hearing. If the defendsnt is found to be guilty of more than half the charges they will be additionally required to pay the plaintiffs legal fee. If the case ends up being dismissed the plaintiff must pay the defendant all legal fees

# **Section 6: Security**
§1 The government is required to station atleast 5 military officers at each court to ensure the security of the court
§2 The state & city is required to secure the area with law enforcement if possible.
§3 During a trial involving politicians the government must deploy 10 additional military officers to the courthouse to secure the area from possible assasination attempts
§4 Any deaths at the hands of deployed military officers securing the court if deemed for the safety of the court is justified otherwise the military officer is to be prosecuted with 1/3 of the normal punishment they are also exempt from any sentence above 25 years in total the government is to pay the military officer once released from prison 1/3 of their usual salary in backpay as they we're deployed by the government with a moderate to high risk of accidental murder

# **Section 7: Trial held by the government**
§1 The minister's scrutiny council may demand a trial held in the national court, they may only sue any part of parliament or political party under the gise of scrutiny such trials must follow standard procedure (section 5) and special procedure in §3 of section 4 
§2 The president's privy council may demand a trial held in the national court procecuting any citizen and any organisation including but not limited to political parties such trials must follow standard procedure (section 5) and special procedure in §3 of section 4 
§3 Unlike civil cases these cases must not have a jury and only a judge if a trial is held by the president's privy council the minister's scrutiny council *must* be present at the trial if possible atleast 2 of the ministers scrutiny council & the presidents privy council members are required to start the trial, they must actively observe the trial and if any corruption is merely suspected the privy council may intervene and order the trial to be postponed and an investigation & trial must be held against the president's privy council held by a different judge. These trials do *not* have a hearing, but will immediately go to trial this said the judge must hear both sides out, their verdict may dismiss the trial entirely and order the defendant to be paid 100 times the amount of minutes spent on the trial & all legal costs these can be paid with tax money. It is good to note that the ministers scrutiny council infact may be absent from the hearing, but is to be shown the logs
