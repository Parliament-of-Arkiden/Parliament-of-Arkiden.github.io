# Section 0: The Constitutional Rights to Have Rights 
All people who step foot into Arkidine Land are entitled to all the rights and freedoms set forth in this Constitutional bill, without distinction of any kind, such as race, colour, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin, property, birth or other status. Any infringement of these rights externally or internally, depending on the right, will lead to the infringer to be indicted 

# Section 1: The Constitutional Right to Life
All people who step foot into Arkidine Land have the right to life, liberty and security of person.

# Section 2: The Constitutional Right to Freedom of Expression and Argumentation
All people who step foot into the Arkidine Land are bestowed the right to freedom of expression and the freedom of speech: people with this right have the ability to present themselves freely, without being persecuted by law unless it infringes upon another person's rights. These freedoms are not limited to but include the following: Freedom of speech, Freedom of the press, Freedom of Belief, Faith and Religion; Freedom of one's own ideologies, Freedom of Identity, and the Freedom of Argumentation.

The Freedom of Argumentation is that of having the ability to defend one's points, even if it has the possibility to be deemed as against another's rights. This right proclaims every person who steps foot on Arkidine Land can be deemed innocent unless there is jurisdiction from the court of law saying otherwise.

# Section 3: The Constitutional Right to Bear Arms
All people who step foot into Arkidine Land have the right to bear arms and the right to use them in defence against non-human creatures who pose a threat to the weapon-bearer's life or for non-life endangering reasons. A person may not use their weapon against another person which threatens their right to life; a person may not use their weapon against another person without consent.

# Section 4: The Constitutional Right to Equality under The Law

All people who step foot into Arkidine Land have the right to recognition as a people before the law. They are all equal under the law and are entitled to be without any discrimination to equal protection of the law. Furthermore, all are entitled to equal protection against any discrimination in violation of the Constitutional Bill of Rights and against any instigation to such discrimination.

# Section 5: The Constitutional Right against Slavery
No person shall be held in slavery, servitude or any other forceful, unpaid, nonconsensual labour.
