# Section 1: What is Parliament?
The parliament of Arkiden (to be referred as “Parliament” henceforth) is a congregation of people who represent areas of the Arkidine populus and are bestowed the power to affect the law of Arkiden.

Parliament is made out of the legislative branch and the executive branch. The executive branch of parliament is tasked with acting out the laws of Arkiden and managing the systems within it. The Legislative branch of Parliament is tasked with creating, critiquing and modifying the laws of the Arkiden along with managing the Executive branch to ensure that the nation is equitably represented and managed. The Executive branch of the parliament of Arkiden must originate from the legislatie branch.

# Section 2: The General Election and the General Assembly
Every parliamentary term, which is 28 days, the current Parliament is dissolved and a General Election is called. During such, an anonymous single-selective poll within all the states’ (excluding New Stoke) group chats will be created to decide the First Ministers -- state leaders -- of each of the states. These polls will have the candidates who have declared for their state of residency; to become a candidate, a person, within their state of residency, must declare they are running for the General Election and want to become a First Minister. The candidate with the most votes becomes the First Minister of their respective state; if there is only one candidate running for the state, that candidate immediately becomes the First Minister at the end of the end of the General Election. Once all First Ministers have been elected, the citizens of Arkiden shall choose who they will want elected as the President of Arkiden. Furthermore, the First Ministers will elect amongst themselves through an anonymous single-selective poll in the First Ministers' group chat, a First Minister to become the Prime Minister. The president may not be elected to become the Prime Minister of Arkiden, however they are allowed to vote on who becomes Prime Minister.
