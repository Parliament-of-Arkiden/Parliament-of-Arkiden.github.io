# Section 1: What is Parliament?
The parliament of Arkiden (to be referred as “Parliament” henceforth) is a congregation of people who represent areas of the Arkidine populace and are bestowed the power to affect the law of Arkiden.
Parliament is made out of the legislative branch and the executive branch. The executive branch of parliament is tasked with acting out the laws of Arkiden and managing the systems within it. The Legislative branch of Parliament is tasked with creating, critiquing and modifying the laws of the Arkiden along with managing the Executive branch to ensure that the nation is equitably represented and managed. The Executive branch of the parliament of Arkiden must originate from the legislative branch.
# Section 2: The General Election and the General Assembly
Every parliamentary term, which is 28 days, the current Parliament is dissolved and a General Election is called. During such, an anonymous single-selective poll within all the states’ (excluding New Stoke) group chats will be created to decide the First Ministers -- state leaders -- of each of the states. These polls will have the candidates who have declared for their state of residency; to become a candidate, a person, within their state of residency, must declare they are running for the General Election and want to become a First Minister. The candidate with the most votes becomes the First Minister of their respective state; if there is only one candidate running for the state, that candidate immediately becomes the First Minister at the end of the end of the General Election. Once all First Ministers have been elected, the citizens of Arkiden shall choose which First Minister they will want elected as the President of Arkiden. Furthermore, the First Ministers will elect amongst themselves through an anonymous single-selective poll in the First Ministers' group chat, a First Minister to become the Prime Minister. The president may not be elected to become the Prime Minister of Arkiden, however they are allowed to vote on who becomes Prime Minister.

After the President and Prime Minister have been chosen, the processes of forming both  The President's Privy Council, the executive branch of parliament; the Prime Minister's Scrutiny committee (to be also referred as the Cabinet and the Committee, respectively), and the General Assembly of Parliament Begins: First, The Prime Minister selects six First Ministers to join them on the Committee, each seat (position) on the Committee must be proportional to the party-share between First Ministers, thus meaning that for one seat on the Committee, including the Prime Minister, must represent three states which are aligned with the same party, furthermore, any leftover seats needed to represent parties (or independents) that control less than three states may be decided on by the Prime Minister, it also doesn't matter if Prime Minister's own seat is not proportional to the party share on the board if they represent a party without three or more First Ministers or are an independent; Secondly, the President will select six First Ministers, who are not a part of the Prime Minister's Committee, to join the President's Cabinet, unlike the Committee, The Cabinet does not need to have members representative to the party-share of First Ministers, in the Cabinet, there must be appointed a Chancellor of the Exchequer and a Chancellor of the Judiciary; Finally, the last seven non-councilmen are known as the Uncommitted Assembly, and when the all twenty-one First Ministers of the parliament are all together, their meeting is known as the general Assembly.

At any time during the parliamentary term, if the general Assembly deems the Prime Minister and their Committee as not beneficial, they may commence a single-selective poll, which shall last for two hours in the Parliament's group chat for the dissolution of the Committee and it's prime minister and to hold another election for the prime ministers. If over three-fifths of the general Assembly (thirteen votes or more) agrees to dissolve the Committee, all First Ministers within it, including the Prime Minister, will become a part of the Uncommitted Assembly and the process of electing a Prime Minister and the process of organising the Committee will start again.

# Section 3: Bills and Processing Legislature
The Parliament as a whole has the divine right to propose, modify, strike down, pass bills. Bills are legal documents which once signed by the respective head of state -- the President or a First Minister(s) -- and the Prime Minister's Committee become law in its place of Jurisdiction. Overall, There are four types of bills: Legislative Bills, Constitutional Bills, Executive Order Bills and Administrative Amendment bills.

A Legislative Bill, is a regular document of law which when passed effects the people and departments of government in its domain. Legislative Bills can be passed on the Federal and on the state level and do not last for a finite period of time unless they have modified or struck down. The process of getting a Legislative Bill passed on the federal level (a federal Legislative Bill) is the following: The bill is proposed by a member of Parliament and is written and then brought up at a General Assembly, The bill is properly discussed, debated and modified during the Assembly, and thus after the bill is properly codified, a single-selective poll within the Parliament's group chat will commence at the end of the Assembly for the the passing of the bill and will last for twenty-four hours; if a majority of the members of Parliament (11 votes or more) agree to the passing of the bill, the bill then has to be agreed upon by the Committee, which if they do via majority of the board agreeing to the bill, the bill will be signed by the Prime Minister on behalf of the Committee and President on the Behalf of the State of Arkiden and officially become the law. The process of getting a Legislative Bill passed on the state level (a statutory Legislative Bill) is the following: A First Minister of the state proposes the bill which will only affect their state, the bill is then shown to the Committee, and the Committee and the First Minister both discuss and debate about how they could both codify the bill; Once an agreement has been made by the two and they want to approve the bill, the First Minister will sign the bill along with Prime Minister on behalf of the Committee, passing the bill into the law of the state.

A Constitutional Bill is a document of law akin to that of Legislative Bill however, by normal legislative means, is immutable. For a regular Legislative Bill to be approved constitutional document, it needs a supermajority of three-quarters of the General Assembly to be passed as such. Furthermore, Constitutional Bills, or modifications to a Constitutional Bill, must be stated in its proposal; A bill in the process of its codifying via the Assembly may not be declared as Constitutional. Constitutional Bills may only take effect on the federal level and cannot be individually passed upon a state.

An Executive Order Bill is a document of law which is similar to a Legislative Bill but it does not need the approval from the General Assembly nor the Committee. An Executive Order Bill can only be proposed by the executive branch of Arkiden -- the President and their privy council -- on the federal level or a First Minister on the statutory level. Unlike Legislative Bills, Executive orders only last during the current parliamentary term, if it is not intervened. During a General Assembly, There is an option to convert an Executive Order Bill into a Legislative Bill, however the processes of approving the bill via majority vote from the assembly and approval from the Committee still apply.

An Administrative Amendment Bill is an addition that could be added to an Executive Order bill and is proposed by the Committee. The Committee are bestowed the power to hold a single-selective poll that will, to decide if an Executive order in effect federally or state-wise should be ratified. If the General Assembly agrees upon the rectification of the Executive Order Bill, The General Assembly will decide on the corrections needed for the bill, however the corrections to the Executive Order Bill will not be directly on it but on a separate document linked to the original bill, the process for approval of this document is the same as a Legislative Bill. Just like an Executive Order Bill, the Administrative Amendment Bill lasts just for the current parliamentary term. If the Executive Order is proposed to become a Legislative Bill but has Administrative Amendment attached to it, The Amendment will override the Executive Order Bill (It will be embedded into the original document and take precedence over the Executive Order Bill) and go through the regular processes foe the Legislative bill.

All documents of Law are to be codified and stored in a external digital collection (external as in out of the current vector of communication such as Whatsapp, Discord or iMessage) and stored according to its federal precedence, if it is national or statutory; and its typing, being Legislative, Constitutional, Executive or Administrative.
