*Executive Order to re-evaluate state autonomy and right to secede*

As President, I would like to declare a special autonomy status for the state of the Adriatic Island given is deeply unique interests and large distance from the mainland. 

If the Adriatic Island wishes to secede from Arkiden, the state owner may choose to do so if a state referendum achieved a simple majority, followed by a Presidential approval.

No Parliamentary voting will be used for the independence of the Adriatic Island.

If other states would like to secede, a parliamentary vote must be held with a simple majority to accept secession.

Secession does not mean a state should be removed from the community. Independence cannot be revoked by a future government.

Signed,
President Daniel
