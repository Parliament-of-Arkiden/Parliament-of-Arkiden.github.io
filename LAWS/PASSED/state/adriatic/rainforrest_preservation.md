*Rainforest Preservation Act*
To preserve the unique biodiversity and beauty of the Adriatic Island's rainforests, this law will enable the government to declare green belts, which are large areas of land where development and resource extraction of any sort is prohibited. Green belts will be in 3 tiers:
Tight Belt - No development of any kind allowed
Normal Belt - Development requires special permission from the government
Loose Belt - Development needs specific considerations to local wildlife
