**JIM ISLANDS - Right to Bear Arms**

This law will give all citizens the right to own weapons, but they must be light arms (mainly firearms).
This is a law is to protect the safety of people of arkiden.
Right to use your weapon:
Citizens will be granted the right to defend themselves with their weapons if their property is being trespassed on without the owner’s permission. The citizen will also be allowed to defend themselves if they receive immediate and unlawful physical attack.
Meaning of threat: a living creature that is attacking the citizen or the citizen's properties physically. 

Before shooting the citizen must warn and at least shout "I'll shoot if you don't stop". If the danger continues, they are granted the right to shoot. 
Those who are deemed mentally unstable, disabled, or ill by the hospital or proper medical care will not have the right to own a weapon. Any weapon should not be stored in the same building as those that are mentally unstable, ill, or disabled. In short:

1.	Ownership restrictions only by court order

2.	Based on documented medical assessment

3.	Right to appeal

Those who are imprisoned for crimes related to violence and in prison for more than 5 years will not be allowed to own any weapons.
Right to carry a weapon in public: You are allowed to transport a weapon in public with the right reason (e.g., taking a gun to the local hunting club, just bought the gun and taking it back home, etc.).
When transporting in public, the ammunition and gun itself must be separated. Carrying a firearm for self-defence in public is illegal

But when defending home? Is legal to defend your own property with a firearm.
