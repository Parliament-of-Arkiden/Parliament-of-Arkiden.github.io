**Pensions Act**

Every citizen at age 65 or after 40 years of work, whichever is shorter, may legally retire and no longer work unless they choose, and will receive a basic state pension of $200 per week. A private pension is applied afterwards depending on the careers of the retiree, and every employer will be required to make a clear pension policy.

To adjust state pension to local purchasing power, state pension will use an index multiplier of purchasing power based on median income.

A median income above the national will have a positive multiplier, while a median income below the national will not be multiplied.

For example, if a state's average income is 25% higher than the national, the state pension will be multiplied by 1.25x, resulting in $250 per week.

Pensions must increase by no less than half of CPI, and will automatically rise by CPI unless a government sets it otherwise

This law applies to all commercial entities, trade ventures, and service providers operating within the territorial boundaries of the Fort Archie. 

Commercial entities operating within the territory are categorized into four distinct tax tiers based on their average monthly gross revenue:
 Tier 1 — Small Business (Under $5,000 / month):
 Tax Rate: 0% (Tax-Exempt)

 Tier 2 — Medium Business ($5,000 to $10,000 / month):
 Tax Rate: 2% flat corporate tax on gross revenues.

 Tier 3 — Large Business ($10,001 to $40,000 / month):
 Tax Rate: 5% flat corporate tax on gross revenues.

 Tier 4 — Extra Large / Enterprise ($40,000+ / month):
 Tax Rate: 7% flat corporate tax on gross revenues.

 Purpose: the tax is to support growing local enterprises while contributing lightly to island upkeep

 Local agricultural innovation
