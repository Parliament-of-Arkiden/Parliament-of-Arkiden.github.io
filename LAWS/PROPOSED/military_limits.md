**Federal Military and State Militia Limits Act**

Type: Federal Legislative Bill
Proposed by: Jasper
Jurisdiction: Federal and State

**Section 1 PURPOSE**
This Act establishes limits on the size of private and state militias and the minimum size for thr Federal Army of Arkiden

**Section 2 STATE AND PRIVATE MILITIAS**

1. Every state and Individual may maintain a private militia.

2. The said militia may have no more then 10,000 active and inactive personnel.

3. The technology of the militia may only have 5 armoured vehicles/weapons , 5 water vehicles/weapons and 3 Air vehicles/weapons. This prohibits Space vehicles/weapons


**Section 3 FEDERAL ARMY**

1. The Federal Army of Arkiden has to have a minimum of 10,000 active personnel, 10 armoured vehicles, 10 water vehicles and 10 air vehicles.
 
2. The Federal Army of Arkiden must swear its allegiance to the people and NO ONE ELSE.

3. The Federal Army shall remain seperate from state or private militias.

**Section 4 ENFORCEMENT**

1. President commands the army and is the commander in chief of the Army until the end of his *Presidential Term* 

2. If the state or private militia goes over the limit the President HAS to act upon it and failure to comply means he forfeits the role of presidency to the opposition party.

3. If the Federal Army Does Not meet requirement the President may not run for a second term
